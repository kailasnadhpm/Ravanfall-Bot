# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the GoldenWave Discord bot and related services ("Service") collect, use, store, and protect information.

## 1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features used, the Service may process:

- Discord user IDs.
- Discord server (guild) IDs.
- Discord channel IDs.
- Discord role IDs.
- Discord message IDs.
- Commands and interaction data.
- Server-specific configuration settings.
- Logs required for troubleshooting, security, moderation, or feature operation.

The Service does not intentionally collect passwords or Discord account credentials.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Provide bot commands and features.
- Store server-specific settings.
- Maintain menus, dashboards, logs, and automated systems.
- Diagnose errors and improve reliability.
- Prevent abuse and protect the Service.
- Provide moderation or administrative features requested by server administrators.

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only when necessary to:

- Operate the Service through hosting or infrastructure providers.
- Comply with legal obligations.
- Protect users, the Service, or others from fraud, abuse, or security threats.

Discord itself processes information according to Discord's own Privacy Policy.

## 4. Data Retention

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, or support configured bot features.

Server configuration or stored data may be removed when it is no longer needed or when the bot is removed from a server, where technically practical.

## 5. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect stored information. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended to be used in accordance with Discord's age requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

## 7. Your Choices and Data Requests

Server owners, administrators, and users may request information about stored data or request deletion where applicable.

Requests can be made through the official support server or contact method provided with the Service.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published at the official policy location.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the bot owner or support team through the official support server or contact method provided with the Service.
