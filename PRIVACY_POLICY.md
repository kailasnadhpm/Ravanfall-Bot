# Ravanfall Bot — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Ravanfall Bot** ("Ravanfall", "we", "our", or the "Service") may collect, process, store, and use information while providing Discord bot features.

## 1. Information Ravanfall May Process

Depending on the features used, Ravanfall Bot may process:

- Discord user IDs
- Discord server (guild) IDs
- Discord channel IDs
- Discord role IDs
- Discord message IDs
- Command and interaction data
- Server configuration settings
- Feature-specific data required for bot functionality
- Error, diagnostic, security, and moderation logs

Ravanfall Bot does **not** intentionally collect Discord passwords or account login credentials.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Provide bot commands and features
- Store server-specific configuration
- Maintain automated menus, dashboards, logs, or systems
- Process administrative and moderation features
- Diagnose errors
- Improve stability and performance
- Prevent abuse
- Protect the bot and its users
- Restore or maintain bot functionality

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to operate Ravanfall Bot through hosting or infrastructure providers, comply with legal requirements, investigate abuse, fraud, or security incidents, or protect users, server owners, the bot, or others.

Discord processes information separately under Discord's own Privacy Policy.

## 4. Data Retention

Ravanfall Bot retains data only for as long as reasonably necessary for bot functionality, server configuration, security, troubleshooting, administrative purposes, or legal obligations.

Data may be removed when it is no longer required, a feature is disabled, the bot is removed from a server, or a valid deletion request is processed.

Some logs or backups may remain temporarily where technically necessary.

## 5. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed by Ravanfall Bot.

However, no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Ravanfall Bot is intended to be used in accordance with Discord's age requirements and applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

## 7. Data Requests

Users, server owners, and administrators may contact the Ravanfall support team to request information about data associated with them or their server, where applicable.

Requests may include:

- Data access questions
- Data deletion requests
- Privacy concerns
- Server-data removal requests

Some requests may require reasonable verification before action is taken.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

The latest version should always be published at the official Privacy Policy URL used for Ravanfall Bot.
